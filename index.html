01 — The Creative Process

The Art of Making

Every piece of pottery begins with clay, water, and an idea. What starts as a simple lump of earth slowly takes shape through the movement of the hands. Whether formed on a pottery wheel or built by hand, the process requires patience, creativity, and an understanding of the material.

As the clay dries, it begins its transformation. Through firing and glazing, its appearance changes, revealing colors, textures, and details that make every piece unique. Each finished piece reflects the decisions of the artist, along with the natural qualities of the clay.

“Every piece begins with a touch.”
